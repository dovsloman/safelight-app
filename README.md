# Safelight

**Capture & markup studio for Windows** — a tray-resident screenshot tool with an
always-editable annotation editor, fully offline OCR, guide animations, and
screen recording.

## Download

Grab **`Safelight-Setup.exe`** from the
[latest release](https://github.com/dovsloman/safelight-app/releases/latest) —
it installs per-user and keeps itself up to date. A portable
`Safelight-win64.zip` is published alongside it, plus SHA-256 checksums for
both.

## What it does

- **Capture**: region (with window snapping and click-a-window), full screen,
  active window, delayed and timed captures, scrolling capture that stitches
  whole pages, multi-monitor capture, and named presets on hotkeys.
- **Annotate**: object-based markup that stays editable — arrows, boxes, text,
  step badges, speech callouts, spotlights, measures, pointer sequences,
  pixelate/blur redaction with an auto-redact scan for emails, phone numbers,
  cards and keys.
- **Guide animations**: annotations reveal step by step and export as GIF,
  PowerPoint slides, or PNG frames — with a mouse pointer that clicks,
  double-clicks, right-clicks and drags its way through your guide.
- **OCR**: 100% offline text extraction; select text straight off a screenshot.
- **Record**: MP4 screen recording with microphone and system audio.
- **Procedures**: capture a task click by click and it becomes a step-by-step
  document — run it on screen, follow it on a phone, or hand it over as Word,
  PDF, slides, a video or a self-contained walkthrough page.
- **Plan markup**: device symbols, cable runs by type, camera coverage from
  the lens and mounting height, a legend and a parts list (CSV).
- **Diagrams**: flowcharts, Gantt charts, sequence, org charts and mind
  maps, signal flow and network, rack elevations, timelines, database and
  state diagrams, and charts — drag them or type them in plain language,
  import and export Mermaid, export to draw.io and Visio, and play them back
  as a GIF or video. For installers: wiring checks, cable labels, rack
  elevations, title blocks, revisions, and a project printed as one set.
- **Draw on the screen**: a pen over whatever is showing, captured with one key.
- **Private by design**: works fully offline, keeps a complete ledger of every
  network request it ever makes, and can be switched to refuse the network
  entirely.
